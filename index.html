The Model

Three steps.
One transformation.

From raw talent in a township to a trusted professional in the PMB market.

01

Train

Every GB-listed professional completes our communication and business readiness programme. This is our quality filter — and what makes our listings trustworthy to the market.

02

List & Connect

GB profiles the professional — skills, pricing, story. We actively connect them to corporate clients, property managers, event planners, and NGOs who need their service.

03

Earn & Scale

The professional earns. GB earns a referral or listing fee. The brand grows. The next professional joins. The cycle compounds — and the empire builds one person at a time.
